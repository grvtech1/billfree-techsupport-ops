Session 3 — Your Role vs Bot's Role
Simple rule: You identify the issue → Bot creates the ticket

🧑‍💼 You — Support Agent

  • ✅ Talk to merchant, understand the problem
  • ✅ Try quick fix on call first
  • ✅ Identify issue: A, B, C, D
  • ✅ Send clear, specific concern to bot
  • ✅ Share Ticket ID with merchant

🤖 BillFree Bot

  • 🤖 Takes your concern and creates a ticket
  • 🤖 Generates a real Ticket ID
  • 🤖 Routes to the right team
  • 🤖 Maintains session memory
  • 🤖 24×7 available — never sleeps 😴
⚠️
Remember: Bot can't do everything on its own. Writing "nothing is working" wastes time. The more specific you are, the faster the bot acts.
